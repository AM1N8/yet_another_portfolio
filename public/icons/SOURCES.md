# Stack icons

The brand symbols in `stack.svg` are selected SVG paths from
[Simple Icons 16.20.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.20.0/icons),
distributed under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
They are served locally and rendered in a single theme-aware monochrome color.
Brand names and logos remain the property of their respective owners.

The `code`, `database`, `network`, `chart`, `vision`, `engineering`, and `coffee`
symbols are original neutral technical glyphs. They support technologies without
a matching Simple Icons asset; they are not presented as official brand marks.
Unrecognized future Stack entries use the `code` glyph alongside their text label.
